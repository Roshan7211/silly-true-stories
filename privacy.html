Silly True Stories

Privacy policy

Last updated 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Silly True Stories Uploader (“the app”), a private tool the owner of the Silly True Stories channel runs on their own computer to publish the channel's own videos. The app has no other users.

What the app accesses

  • YouTube: with the owner's permission, the app uses YouTube API Services to upload videos and set their title, description, visibility and schedule on the owner's own channel. It does not read viewers' data, comments, subscribers or watch history.
  • Instagram: with the owner's permission, the app uses the Instagram API to read the owner's own account name and ID and to publish Reels to that account. It does not read followers', commenters' or other people's data.

Where data is kept

The sign-in tokens Google and Meta issue are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database, and it does not collect, sell or share any personal data with anyone. While a Reel is being posted, the video file is made briefly available at a temporary web address so Instagram can fetch it, and it is removed afterwards.

Google and Meta

By using the app you are also covered by the Google Privacy Policy and the Meta Privacy Policy. The app's use of YouTube API Services follows the YouTube Terms of Service.

Removing access and deleting data

Access can be withdrawn at any time: for Google at myaccount.google.com/connections, and for Instagram under Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites. Deleting the app's token files from the computer removes everything the app stores. Nothing is kept anywhere else.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
