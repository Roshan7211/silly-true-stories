Silly True Stories

Funny true stories from around the world

Silly True Stories makes short animated videos about real events that sound made up: the bridge that wobbled on its opening day, the morning a whole country swapped which side of the road it drove on, and more. A new story most days, each about a minute long.

Find us as @SillyTrueStories on YouTube: youtube.com/@SillyTrueStories.

Silly True Stories Uploader

The Silly True Stories Uploader is a small private tool the channel's owner runs on their own computer to post the channel's finished videos to its own YouTube channel and Instagram account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users. It uses YouTube API Services and the Instagram API.
